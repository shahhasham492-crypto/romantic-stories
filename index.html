The CEO and the Village Girl

Chapter 1 • A Chance Encounter

The rain had turned the village road into a ribbon of silver when Ayesha hurried home, holding her shawl tightly around her shoulders. She had no idea that this ordinary evening would change her life forever.

At the edge of the road, a black luxury car stood silently beside a broken wooden fence. A man in a dark suit stepped out, looking completely out of place among the quiet fields and small houses.

He was Arham Malik, a successful CEO who had come to the village for a business matter. He was used to boardrooms, deadlines and people who wanted something from him. He was not used to someone simply asking, “Are you alright?”

Sometimes love does not arrive with a warning. Sometimes it simply appears on a rainy road.

Ayesha offered him her umbrella. Arham looked at her for a moment, surprised by the warmth in her eyes. He did not know her name yet, but somehow he already knew he would remember this meeting.
